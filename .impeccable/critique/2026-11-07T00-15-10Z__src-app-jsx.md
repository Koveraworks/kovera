### Overall Impression
Kovera has a remarkably grounded, empathetic mission and a disciplined design token structure, but is held back by one critical operational omission (no phone/WhatsApp collection), mobile cognitive overload (stacking both registration forms simultaneously), and disruptive native `alert()` validation.

### What's Working
1. **The "4-Hour Problem" Grounding:** Replaces generic gig-economy abstraction with the immediate operational pain of shop owners (weekend rushes, sudden illness).
2. **Dual-Sided Marketplace Symmetry:** Strict, deliberate separation between Deep Civic Indigo (`#302F80`) and Kinetic Worker Teal (`#18A999`).
3. **Interactive Shift Explorer Bridge:** Seamlessly connects role exploration to pre-filled registration, cutting form paralysis.

### Priority Issues

#### [P0] Missing Phone Number / WhatsApp Contact Field
- **Why it matters:** In Panipat, shift coordination happens 100% via phone call or WhatsApp. Collecting only names and general locality leaves Kovera with zero reachability to coordinate matches.
- **Fix:** Add a required phone input with `+91` prefix and helper text: "We coordinate shift availability via WhatsApp / Phone".
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden src/App.jsx`

#### [P1] Intrusive Native `window.alert()` Form Validation
- **Why it matters:** Form validation uses `alert(...)`, which freezes mobile browser execution, harms brand trust, and breaks screen readers.
- **Fix:** Replace `alert()` with inline error states (red border `#EF4444` and helper text below each offending input).
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify src/App.jsx`

#### [P1] Dual Form Stacking on Mobile Viewports (Cognitive Overload)
- **Why it matters:** Defaulting to "Show Both" forms (`activeFormTab === 'both'`) forces mobile users to scroll through 8 inputs and 2 separate submit buttons. Visitors are either a merchant or a worker, never both.
- **Fix:** Default tab to `'business'` or inherit the Hero CTA intent, and hide the "Both" tab option on mobile screens.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout src/App.jsx`

#### [P2] Off-System Sub-12px Micro-Typography (9 Instances)
- **Why it matters:** 9 instances of arbitrary `text-[10px]` and `text-[11px]` violate `DESIGN.md` and degrade legibility on low-cost mobile displays.
- **Fix:** Standardize all 9 occurrences to `text-xs` (12px, font-weight 700).
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable typeset src/App.jsx`

#### [P2] Sci-Fi Hero Illustration Clashing with Civic Identity
- **Why it matters:** The glowing holographic command deck clashes with the "Civic Dispatch" street-level reality of Insar Bazaar and Model Town.
- **Fix:** Replace with an authentic daylight dispatch receipt card (e.g. "Boutique Support · Model Town · Today 2PM–6PM · Matched").
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable bolder src/App.jsx`
