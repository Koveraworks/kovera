#### Overall Impression
Kovera has achieved rare authenticity for an on-demand marketplace: it feels like a real civic infrastructure product built specifically for Panipat shopkeepers and local workers. The engineering and accessibility floors are exceptionally solid. However, the page suffers from **content bloat and cognitive overload**: it forces users through four back-to-back interactive calculators/simulators and 16+ waitlist buttons, diluting its most powerful proposition.

#### What's Working
1. **Exceptional Local Grounding**: The hyper-specific corridor mapping (Insar Bazaar, Model Town, HUDA) and localized retail role tasks (saree folding, trial room lines, cafe rushes) create immediate local credibility.
2. **Dual-Sided Marketplace Symmetry**: The disciplined Indigo (business) vs. Teal (worker) color system provides consistent cognitive orientation throughout, backed by transparent ₹0 worker fee assurances.
3. **Defensive Form & Motion Engineering**: State and City separation, auto-formatted 10-digit Indian mobile validation with `+91` prefix, ARIA error bindings, `Escape` key handlers, and GPU-composited RAF scroll indicators provide a native-app feel.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Cognitive Overload: Four Consecutive Interactive Modules & Redundant Section 05**
  - **Why it matters**: Users are forced to navigate the Dispatch Simulator, Shift Explorer, Compensation Calculator, and Coverage Radar back-to-back, followed by Section 05 (`#marketplace`) which re-explains basic concepts already demonstrated in the hero. This causes cognitive exhaustion before reaching registration.
  - **Fix**: Remove Section 05 (`#marketplace`) entirely; consolidate the Shift Explorer and Shift Calculator into a single unified "Shift & Rate Explorer" module.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill`

- **[P1] Disorienting 1,500px Scroll Jump on Preset CTA Clicks**
  - **Why it matters**: Tapping "Request this support" or role chips scrolls the user 15 screens down to `#waitlist`. Distracted mobile users lose spatial orientation, cannot verify what was imported, and cannot return to where they were reading.
  - **Fix**: Render an inline dismissible "Active Preset" badge directly above the waitlist form (e.g., `📍 Selected: Boutique Support · 4 Hours · Model Town [Clear / Return to Top]`) providing continuity.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt`

- **[P2] Inaccessible Teal Text Contrast on Light Canvas Surfaces**
  - **Why it matters**: Brand Teal `#18A999` on white (`#FFFFFF`) or pale teal (`#E4F7F3`) yields a contrast ratio of only **~2.93:1**, failing WCAG AA (minimum 4.5:1). Under outdoor sunlight in Panipat, earnings estimates and category subtitles wash out completely.
  - **Fix**: Replace text instances of `#18A999` on light backgrounds with Deep Worker Teal (`#0E6E63` or `#128679`, yielding >4.5:1). Reserve `#18A999` strictly for solid button fills, active dot accents, and borders.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable colorize`

- **[P2] Buried Institutional Trust Signals for High-Anxiety Personas**
  - **Why it matters**: Tier-2 retail workers (students, homemakers) and boutique owners have high skepticism regarding scams and safety. Pushing Trust & Safety commitments (18+ ID verified, daytime only, public storefronts only, ₹0 worker fee) down to Section 07 means high-intent visitors drop off before seeing safety guarantees.
  - **Fix**: Pin safety badges directly inside the hero subtitle area and embed trust verification points directly inside the Worker waitlist card.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

- **[P3] Dead Code & Sub-Standard Mobile Touch Targets**
  - **Why it matters**: `src/App.css` (184 lines) is an unreferenced leftover file from the default Vite template that triggers lint warnings. Additionally, interactive scenario switcher pills (`lines 878–891`) measure ~28px in height, falling below the 44px mobile touch target standard.
  - **Fix**: Delete `src/App.css`; expand interactive pill padding to `min-h-[44px]`.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`
